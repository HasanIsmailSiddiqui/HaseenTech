// ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

Terms & Conditions.

Clear, professional ground rules governing client proposals, milestone payments, intellectual property ownership, and project delivery.

1. Engagement & Scope Alignment

All development work commences upon written agreement to an itemized proposal detailing agreed page counts, functionalities, milestones, and deliverable timelines. Scope expansions outside the agreed proposal are quoted transparently as add-ons.

2. Payment Structure

Standard project engagements require a 50% mobilization deposit before discovery and architecture begin, with the final 50% balance payable upon completion of QA and prior to live production DNS handover.

3. Intellectual Property Rights

Upon settlement of the final invoice, full intellectual property and copyright for custom frontend templates, custom CSS stylesheets, and client-provided assets transfer entirely to the client. Third-party plugins remain subject to their respective open-source or commercial licenses.

4. Client Content & Third-Party Services

Clients retain full ownership of their text, imagery, and branding assets and warrant that they possess necessary rights for their use. Hosting fees, domain renewals, premium plugin licenses, and payment processor transaction fees are paid directly to those respective third-party providers.

5. Governing Law & Inquiries

For any questions regarding our service agreements, contact hello@haseentech.com.
