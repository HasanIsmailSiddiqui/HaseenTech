1. Agreement to Terms

By engaging HaseenTech (founded by Hasan Ismail) for web development, design, maintenance, or consulting services, you agree to the terms outlined below. These terms are designed to ensure fair, transparent, and predictable collaboration.

2. Scope of Work & Project Proposals

All development projects are governed by a mutually agreed proposal or statement of work detailing page count, features, milestones, and deliverable specifications. Any feature, third-party integration, or page request not explicitly included in the written proposal constitutes out-of-scope work and will be quoted separately before implementation.

3. Client Responsibilities

To maintain agreed project timelines, the client agrees to:

  • Provide page copy, brand guidelines, logos, and high-resolution assets in a timely manner.
  • Grant necessary technical credentials (such as web hosting or domain DNS access) when required.
  • Review milestone deliverables and provide consolidated feedback within agreed review windows.

4. Revisions Policy

Each package includes structured revision rounds (typically 2 rounds for Landing Pages, 3 rounds for Business and WooCommerce projects). A revision round consists of a consolidated checklist of adjustments to styling, layout spacing, and copy placement. Revisions do not include fundamental structural changes to previously approved sitemaps or scopes.

5. Web Hosting, Domains & Third-Party Services

Clients retain direct ownership and financial responsibility for third-party accounts, including web hosting servers, domain registration renewals, commercial font/plugin licenses, and payment gateway transaction fees. HaseenTech does not mark up third-party hosting or hold client accounts hostage.

6. Intellectual Property & Deliverable Ownership

Upon receipt of full and final payment, complete ownership rights to custom website code, Elementor templates, graphics, and page styling transfer exclusively to the client. Pre-existing open-source code libraries (such as WordPress Core and WooCommerce) remain governed by their respective open-source licenses.

We build websites adhering to modern engineering standards, responsive best practices, and Core Web Vitals hygiene. However, no legitimate digital agency can guarantee specific Google search ranking positions, lead volumes, or advertising conversions, as these outcomes depend on competitive search algorithms and market factors beyond any single engagement.
