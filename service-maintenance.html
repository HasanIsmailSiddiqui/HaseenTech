The Hidden Risks of Neglected Sites

Security Vulnerabilities from Outdated Plugins

Over 80% of WordPress security breaches originate from known vulnerabilities in unpatched plugins. Regular updates protect your data.

White Screen of Death After Careless Updates

Clicking "Update All" in the WordPress dashboard without testing can crash your live layout or break critical payment gateways. We stage everything first.

Host Outages with No Verified Backups

If your server corrupts without verified recent backups, recreating your site from scratch costs thousands. We safeguard off-site restore points.
