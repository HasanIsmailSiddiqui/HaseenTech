Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Governed by HaseenTech

1. Overview and Commitment

HaseenTech, founded by Hasan Ismail and based in Karachi, Pakistan ("we", "us", "our"), respects your personal privacy. This policy outlines how we handle information collected through our website (https://haseentech.com) and during client project communications.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to evaluate your project and maintain business communications:

  • Direct Inquiries: Your name, email address, company name, location/country, and project details submitted through our contact form, direct email (hello@haseentech.com), or WhatsApp.
  • Local Browser Preferences: We use client-side browser localStorage to store your selected market/currency preference (e.g. USD, GBP, PKR) to ensure a seamless pricing display on return visits. This storage contains zero identifying personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your provided information solely to:

  • Evaluate your website requirements and prepare honest project scopes and quotations.
  • Communicate milestone deliverables, staging previews, and handover assets.
  • Fulfill mutually agreed contractual agreements and project milestones.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize client personal data. Your information is never transferred to data brokers or advertising networks. We only interact with trusted third-party services that you explicitly authorize for your project (such as your chosen web hosting provider or payment gateway).

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard security hygiene to safeguard communication records. Client briefs and project files are retained for the duration of our engagement and reasonable record-keeping periods, after which they can be securely archived or deleted upon request.

6. Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located (including the EU, UK, US, or Pakistan), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your records. To exercise these rights, please email us directly at hello@haseentech.com.
