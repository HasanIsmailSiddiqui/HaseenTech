When you submit a project inquiry or contact us via email, we collect your name, business email address, company location, and project requirements. We only collect information that you explicitly choose to share with us for communication purposes.

We use your submitted details exclusively to: (1) Review your project requirements and prepare quotations; (2) Communicate regarding development milestones, deliverable handovers, and invoices; (3) Provide administrative walkthroughs and ongoing support.
