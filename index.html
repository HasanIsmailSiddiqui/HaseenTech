Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers about our working methods, pricing policies, and scope boundaries.

Do you work with international clients?
Yes. HaseenTech collaborates remotely with businesses and agencies across the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Canada, Australia, Europe, and Pakistan. We coordinate milestones using clear asynchronous communication and scheduled video calls.
What do you need from me to start a project?
A brief overview of your business, your page outline or content draft, brand assets (logo and colors), and hosting or domain access when applicable. If you do not yet have copy ready, we can assist with wireframe placeholders to guide your team.
Are package prices fixed or customizable?
The rates listed in our pricing section serve as fixed baseline investments for the predefined scope (page count and features). If your project requires additional integrations, multi-language setups, or customized features, we provide a detailed written proposal before any work begins.
Do you provide hosting and domains?
Domain registration and web hosting accounts are owned and billed directly to you for security and ownership integrity. We provide trusted hosting recommendations (e.g. Cloudways, Hostinger, SiteGround) and handle the full deployment and DNS configuration on your account.
How do revisions work?
Each package includes dedicated structured revision rounds (2 rounds for Starter, 3 for Business and WooCommerce). A revision round consists of a consolidated checklist of adjustments to layouts, typography, imagery, and copy. Additional rounds can be scoped if your requirements change.
Can you guarantee #1 Google rankings or speed scores?
No agency can legitimately guarantee a #1 Google ranking or an arbitrary speed score, as search algorithms, hosting infrastructure, and third-party scripts continuously change. We implement proven on-page technical SEO hygiene, clean semantic markup, fast caching, and image compression to give your site the strongest possible technical foundation.
