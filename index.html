// COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear, honest answers about our technical capabilities, pricing, and project workflows.

Do you offer hosting, domain registration, and email accounts?
Hosting and domain registrations are billed directly by your preferred provider (such as Hostinger, SiteGround, Cloudways, or Namecheap) so you retain 100% ownership and administrative access. We provide clear provider recommendations and handle deployment and configuration for you.
Can my team edit website pages, copy, and images after launch?
Yes. All our WordPress and WooCommerce builds are configured with visual editing controls. We provide a concise walkthrough and recorded training notes so you can update text, blog posts, case studies, and products without writing code.
How does international communication and payment work?
We collaborate asynchronously via email, Loom video walkthroughs, and scheduled WhatsApp or video calls across North American, European, and Gulf time zones. Invoicing is conducted with milestone terms via secure international wire or digital transfer.
Do you guarantee specific Google keyword rankings or sales numbers?
No ethical agency guarantees search rankings or revenue figures because search algorithms and market demand are external variables. What we guarantee is rigorous technical execution: proper schema markup, semantic headings, fast load times, and conversion-focused UI design.
Can you take over or fix an existing website built by another team?
Yes. We conduct a preliminary technical review to inspect your hosting, theme structure, and active plugins. If viable, we formulate an action plan for maintenance, redesign, or speed remediation.
