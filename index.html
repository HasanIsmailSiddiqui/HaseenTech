Working Method

A Predictable, Transparent Process

Every project follows five clear stages with defined milestones, structured revision rounds, and honest timelines.

Step 01

Discovery

We align on commercial objectives, page sitemaps, required integrations, and agree on a fixed scope agreement.

Step 02

Architecture

Step 03

Development

Step 04

Refinement

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers regarding how we scope, quote, and deliver remote client projects.

Do you work with international clients outside Pakistan?
Yes. HaseenTech works remotely with businesses and agencies across the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Canada, Australia, and Europe. We align on milestones, async communication tools (Slack, email, Loom), and scheduled video calls that match your timezone.
What do you need from me to begin a project?
A brief summary of your project goals, your page outline or content draft, your logo/brand assets, and hosting or domain access if applicable. If you do not yet have copy finalized, we can provide structural wireframes to help your team prepare it.
Are your package prices fixed or customizable?
The prices shown represent baseline initial proposals for predefined page counts and standard scopes. If your project involves custom plugins, CRM integrations, multilingual setup, or specific API feeds, we provide a transparent written quotation before kickoff.
Do you supply web hosting and domain registration?
Hosting and domain registration are owned and billed directly by you to ensure you retain complete legal and technical ownership of your website. We can recommend reputable providers (e.g. Cloudways, Hostinger) and handle the full deployment and DNS configuration on your account.
How do revisions work?
Each package includes structured revision rounds (2 rounds for Landing Pages, 3 rounds for Business and WooCommerce sites). A revision round consists of a consolidated list of feedback regarding styling, spacing, typography, and copy alignment. Additional rounds can be quoted if your requirements expand.
