Common E-Commerce Obstacles

Friction-Heavy Cart Abandonment

Cluttered checkouts with confusing form fields cause customers to leave before completing purchases. We streamline checkout to minimal essential steps.

Complex Product Attribute Breakage

When sizes, colors, and variations are misconfigured, stock levels desynchronize and customers get confused. We structure robust attribute frameworks.

Payment Gateway Failures

Misconfigured webhook listeners or currency conflicts result in lost revenue. We configure and test gateways end-to-end prior to launch.
