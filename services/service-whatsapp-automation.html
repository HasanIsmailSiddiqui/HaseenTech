WhatsApp Chatbots & Workflow Automation.

Turn your most active customer communication channel into an automated growth engine. We design rule-based and AI-powered WhatsApp chatbots integrated with your CRM, Google Sheets, and eCommerce order systems.

// BOT ARCHITECTURES

Rule-Based vs. AI Bots

  • Rule-Based Menu Bots: Fast, deterministic button menus and keyword triggers for instant catalog browsing, branch locations, and pricing tiers without conversational ambiguity.
  • AI-Powered WhatsApp Bots: Natural language understanding capable of answering unstructured customer queries using your business FAQs and policies.
  • Automated Human Handover: Instant routing to live human agents on your WhatsApp Business app or multi-agent inbox when critical thresholds are reached.
// WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Connected Integrations

  • Instant lead sync from WhatsApp into Google Sheets, Notion, HubSpot, or Airtable
  • WooCommerce order confirmation and tracking lookup via WhatsApp API
  • Automated appointment scheduling reminders and confirmation messages
  • Official WhatsApp Cloud API & Meta Business verification assistance
